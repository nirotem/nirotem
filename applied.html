Applied Social Research & Policy Evaluation

Over a decade of experience designing and executing program evaluations, large-scale survey research, and mixed-methods studies for public agencies, philanthropic foundations, and research institutes. Bridging advanced statistical modeling and qualitative inquiry with actionable strategic recommendations.

Core Competencies & Toolkit

Quantitative Analysis & Modeling

  • Statistical Programming: Advanced proficiency in Stata; data management, regression modeling, and longitudinal panel analysis.
  • Predictive Analytics: Applied machine learning classification (decision trees, neural networks) for institutional retention and demographic forecasting.
  • Survey Methodology: Survey instrument design, sampling frameworks, and multivariate attitudinal analysis.

Qualitative & Mixed Methods

  • Primary Qualitative Fieldwork: In-depth interviews and focus group facilitation.
  • Thematic & Textual Coding: Qualitative data analysis and coding utilizing NVivo and ATLAS.ti.
  • Policy Translation: Executive report authoring, stakeholder presentations, policy briefs, and actionable intervention design.

Selected Evaluation & Applied Research Projects

Program Evaluation & Educational Policy

The Henrietta Szold Institute | Research Fellow & Qualitative Lead (2018–2026)

Led qualitative research tracks across multiple large-scale educational program evaluation projects. Designed evaluation protocols, conducted fieldwork including extensive stakeholder interviews and focus groups across diverse demographic communities, executed rigorous thematic coding, and authored comprehensive final evaluation reports with concrete, evidence-based recommendations for educational leaders and policymakers.

Institutional Analytics, Retention & Equity

Hebrew University of Jerusalem | Co-Principal Investigator

Conducted an extensive empirical investigation into higher education persistence using decade-long institutional administrative data. Modeled risk factors for undergraduate and graduate student attrition, identified the phenomenon of "existential dropouts" among students in good academic standing, and evaluated the long-term graduation trajectories of students admitted via affirmative action pathways to demonstrate that expanded access maintains institutional retention standards.

Global Demographic Data Analytics

Institute for Social Research and Data Innovation (ISRDI), University of Minnesota | Data Analyst & Researcher

Executed advanced statistical analysis on large-scale international demographic, population, and health surveys. Cleaned and structured complex multi-level microdata to evaluate social equity, health behaviors, and the localized enactment of global policy standards.
